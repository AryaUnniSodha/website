Campaign Discovery

Distinct initiative cards help visitors find a cause and take the next step.

3

Verified Impact Metrics

Volunteer Registration

Community Stories & Testimonials

Why these features matter

Donation transparencyVisitors give more readily when they see where funds go.
Campaign discoveryClear initiatives show people exactly what they can support.
Verified impactPublished, confirmed outcomes earn long-term trust.
Volunteer registrationAn easy sign-up grows the community of helpers.
Community storiesReal voices, shared with consent, show the human result.

Prototype only: no payments are processed and no form data is sent. Contact details and registration information are to be confirmed by the foundation.
